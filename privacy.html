Privacy policy

Home Organizer. Effective 20 October 2026.

Home Organizer is a private tool used by one household. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with data from Google.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: free/busy times and the titles and times of events on the calendars its owner connects; creating the events its owner asks for; reading back, updating and removing only the events the app itself created.
  • Gmail: message metadata only (labels and change history), to notice that new mail has arrived. It does not read the content of messages through this access.

How it is used

Only to show the owner their own schedule, find free time, add calendar entries they request, and notice new mail. Some of this information may be processed by an AI assistant running under the owner's own account, only to prepare suggestions for the owner.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is kept only on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • Home Organizer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Home Organizer's access at any time in their Google Account settings. Data kept on the owner's computer can be deleted there at any time.

Contact

Through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.

  • Gmail: נתוני מעטפת בלבד (תוויות והיסטוריית שינויים), כדי לדעת שהגיע מייל חדש. תוכן ההודעות לא נקרא דרך הגישה הזו.
